Purpose

Ace’s Support Command turns technology work into human-centered support. Students learn to listen, record the issue, take action, verify the fix, and close the loop.

Student verbs

Listen, document, resolve, confirm

Current state

This zone connects to the ACE Service Desk proposal and future GLPI service on Rocket’s Forge. It will support simulated help desk tickets and student support workflows.
