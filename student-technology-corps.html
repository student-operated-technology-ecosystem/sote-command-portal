Legacy route

The Student Technology Corps role model has been retired.

This URL is retained so old links do not break, but the former Technology Cadet → Technician → Mission Control Operator progression is no longer the SOTE organizational model.

Current model

SOTE now separates governance, persistent operations, and bounded mission execution: Mission Command, SOTE Operations, and SOTE Missions. Students may serve as Mission Technicians when assigned to approved work. SOTE Operators are persistent operational roles. Course enrollment alone does not confer SOTE authority.
