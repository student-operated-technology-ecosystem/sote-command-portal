ACE-TRAIN-001

Read the ticket evidence.

Here is the entire training ticket. You do not need to search for another document.

USER REPORT“This computer says it is connected, but websites will not load.”

Environment: fictional classroom Windows workstation using a wired network connection.

What changed: the desk area was cleaned and cables were moved earlier that morning.

Observed evidence: the workstation powers on normally. The network icon shows a wired connection. Nearby classroom workstations can reach websites.

Safe checks already performed: the Ethernet cable is visibly connected at the workstation. No equipment has been opened, reset, or reconfigured.

Training resolution evidence: after reseating the accessible Ethernet connection at the workstation, network access returned. A test website loaded successfully.

Your job

Document only what the evidence supports. Do not invent a root cause that was never proven.

Think first

Turn evidence into technician guidance.

Before touching GitHub, decide what belongs in the article.

WITH THE CLASS

Identify:

  • two clarification questions you would ask the user;
  • the safest first observable check;
  • what action appears to have restored service;
  • how you would verify the problem is resolved;
  • one condition that should cause a technician to stop and escalate.
Important distinction

“Reseating the connection restored service” is supported. “The cable was definitely loose” is not proven. Good documentation separates evidence from assumption.

# Workstation Connected but Websites Will Not Load

**Status:** Student Draft — not approved operational guidance
**Scenario:** ACE-TRAIN-001
**Applies to:** Fictional classroom Windows workstation using a wired network connection

## Problem and Symptoms

The user reports that the workstation appears connected to the network, but websites will not load. Nearby classroom workstations can reach websites.

## Questions to Ask

- When did the problem begin?
- Did anything change or move around the workstation before the problem started?

## Before You Begin

Obtain permission before moving cables or interrupting the user's work. Do not open equipment, reset network hardware, or change network configuration during this classroom exercise.

## Troubleshooting Steps

1. Confirm the workstation powers on normally.
2. Observe the network status shown by the workstation.
3. Compare the symptom with another nearby classroom workstation.
4. Visually inspect the accessible Ethernet connection at the workstation.
5. If permitted, reseat the accessible Ethernet connection without forcing or removing secured equipment.
6. Test access to an instructor-approved website.

## Likely Resolution

Network access returned after the accessible Ethernet connection at the workstation was reseated. The evidence does not prove why the connection failed.

## Verification

Confirm that the workstation can load an instructor-approved website and that the user can resume normal network use.

## Stop and Escalate

Stop if equipment appears damaged, a step requires elevated permission, network configuration changes are required, or the safe checks do not restore access. Record the symptoms, checks, and results for the next technician.
