Explore SOTE

One ecosystem. Multiple technology worlds.

SOTE is a student-operated, mission-based technology environment where classrooms connect to real operational practice. Students learn by observing systems, building infrastructure, supporting users, defending environments, documenting what they discover, and improving the ecosystem for the next crew.
