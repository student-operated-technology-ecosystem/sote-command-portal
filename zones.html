Legacy route

The old named “Zones” are now treated as SOTE work areas.

Numbered Zones 0–3 are reserved for the SOTE Trust Architecture. Names such as Mission Control, Ace Help Desk, Rocket's Engineering Bay, and Patch Bay Alpha describe functional areas, not authority zones.
