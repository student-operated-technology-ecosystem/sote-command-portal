Knowledge article standard

Operational articles use a consistent structure so technicians know what they are allowed to do, what success looks like, what evidence to preserve, and when to stop.

Purpose & scope

What the article teaches, where it applies, and what authorization is required.

Stop & escalate

Explicit boundaries for safety, destructive actions, uncertainty, and production impact.

Procedure & results

Execution-order steps plus expected and alternate states.

Evidence

Outputs, screenshots, measurements, logs, or notes that make the work defensible.

Troubleshooting

Safe recovery guidance without inviting improvisation beyond authorization.

Version history

Related missions, runbooks, knowledge, and a traceable history of accepted changes.
