Classroom boundary

The CIT-205 repository is public. Use fictionalized scenario facts only. Never publish credentials, private tickets, student records, real user information, internal network details, or private screenshots.

Classroom participation does not make you a SOTE Operator or grant infrastructure authority.

What counts as complete?

Your mission produces six-scenario clarification questions, one Word draft, one Markdown Knowledge Article pull request, one actionable peer review, one revision responding to feedback, and a short explanation of which copy is current and how GitHub proves it.

Knowledge article standard

Operational articles use a consistent structure so technicians know what they are allowed to do, what success looks like, what evidence to preserve, and when to stop.

Purpose & scope

What the article teaches, where it applies, and what authorization is required.

Stop & escalate

Explicit boundaries for safety, destructive actions, uncertainty, and production impact.

Procedure & results

Execution-order steps plus expected and alternate states.

Evidence

Outputs, screenshots, measurements, logs, or notes that make the work defensible.

Troubleshooting

Safe recovery guidance without inviting improvisation beyond authorization.

Version history

Related missions, runbooks, knowledge, and a traceable history of accepted changes.
