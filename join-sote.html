Join the SOTE interest list

SOTE welcomes students interested in help desk operations, networking, cybersecurity, Linux, documentation, validation, and technology projects. You do not need prior professional IT experience.

Submitting an interest form is not an application for employment, a promise of a paid position, or a guarantee of participation. We will review each student's interests and explore appropriate learning, project, and, where authorized, student employment pathways. Federal work-study eligibility and other employment arrangements are determined through applicable college processes.

What we'll ask

  1. Full name
  2. CCAC email address
  3. Current program or area of study (optional)
  4. Mission areas of interest (select any)
  5. Why would you like to participate in SOTE?
  6. What courses, projects, experiences, or interests would you bring? No prior experience required.
  7. What would you most like to learn?
  8. General availability
  9. Which semester(s) interest you? Spring 2027, Summer 2027, or future opportunities
  10. Would you like to hear about potential paid student employment pathways, project participation, or both?
  11. Acknowledgment that this is an interest form, not an employment application

What happens next?

Mission Command reviews interest submissions, considers possible mission assignments, and may contact students for a conversation. Any paid position requires separate college authorization and eligibility review.
