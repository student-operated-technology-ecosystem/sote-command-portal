What counts as complete?

A component should be inventoried, located, connected, addressed, reproducibly configured, documented, recoverable, role-owned, discoverable, and independently validated as applicable. The goal is not green boxes. The goal is an environment that a new operator can inherit without needing the original builder standing beside them.
