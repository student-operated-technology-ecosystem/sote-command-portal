Organization

Mission Command directs. Operations operates. Missions execute bounded work.

SOTE separates governance, persistent operations, and mission execution so authority survives student turnover and never depends on informal access.

Mission Command

Mission Director & Associate Mission Directors

Strategy, architecture governance, portfolio priority, partnerships, resources, risk acceptance, and exceptional authority.

SOTE Operations

SOTE Operators

Persistent operational staff who maintain approved infrastructure, services, documentation, validation, and continuity.

SOTE Missions

Mission Technicians

Students participating in approved bounded missions. Enrollment alone does not confer SOTE authority.

Authority is explicit

Physical scope, infrastructure scope, service scope, and mission scope determine what someone may actually do. Titles never imply unlimited access.
