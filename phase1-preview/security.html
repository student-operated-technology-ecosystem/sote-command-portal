Security Architecture

Transparency without accessibility.

ProfessorSneaker is intentionally public. Authentication begins only where a user crosses into protected information, protected systems, or privileged actions.

Public

Information

Classroom materials, KB articles, missions, architecture, sanitized infrastructure, service purpose, and continuity status can remain public.

Privilege boundary

Authenticate where authority begins

GitHub, GLPI, NetBox, Proxmox, firewalls, switches, and other protected systems keep their own identity and authorization controls.

Blast radius

Portal compromise stops at the portal

If ProfessorSneaker is completely compromised, the attacker gets the portal and public-safe data. They do not get SOTE.

Public read

Default portal experience

No ProfessorSneaker account is required for ordinary browsing and learning.

Low-risk write

Anonymous actions can exist carefully

Ticket intake may be allowed with validation, rate limiting, logging, and narrowly scoped backend permissions.

Privileged action

Use the real system's authentication

Changes to protected systems happen behind GitHub, GLPI, or another authoritative identity boundary, not a homegrown portal account.
