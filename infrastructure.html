Current build priority

Mission Control → Rack 1 → Rack 2

The current critical path is establishing reliable classroom access through Mission Control and Rack 1 into Rack 2, then launching foundational services.

1

Mission Control aggregation

Recommission and document the aggregation pathway without placing the backbone under normal classroom control.

2

Rack 1 instructional access

Networking 2 students can reach and configure designated Cisco equipment within explicit mission scope.

3

Rack 2 production path

Extend the validated pathway into production switching and the initial production subnet.

4

Foundational services

DHCP, DNS, GLPI, and later additional production services become reachable from authorized classroom systems.
