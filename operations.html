Portfolio discipline

Not every good idea becomes active work.

Active

Current execution

Work consuming operator time and tied to current milestones.

Approved / Queued

Authorized next work

Approved missions waiting for prerequisites, capacity, or scheduled execution.

Discovery

Investigate first

Work intended to determine whether a full implementation mission should exist.

Future / Backlog

Preserve the idea

Strategically useful work that does not currently deserve execution capacity.

Blocked

Named dependency

Work that cannot proceed until an identified prerequisite or approval is resolved.
