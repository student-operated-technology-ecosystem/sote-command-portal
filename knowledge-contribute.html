What happens next

  1. Open this exact article in GitHub.
    You do not need to hunt through the repository.
  2. Choose Edit.
    If GitHub needs a fork or branch for your account, let it create one. Do not edit the canonical main branch directly.
  3. Make the smallest useful correction.
    Preserve the article structure and leave the next operator something clearer than you found it.
  4. Commit the change to your branch.
    Use a short message that says what you corrected.
  5. Open a pull request.
    Review Files changed, explain why the change improves the article, and submit it for review.
