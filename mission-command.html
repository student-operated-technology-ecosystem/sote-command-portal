SOTE Mission Command

Here is the problem. Engineer the solution.

Mission Command turns authentic technology needs into student-engineered solutions. Explore open missions, investigate the problem, submit a defensible proposal, and follow approved work into execution.

Mission lifecycle

From support need to operational handoff

Help Desk Ticket → Mission Candidate → Open for Proposals → Proposal → Mission Command Review → Approved Mission Backlog → Mission Fund / Resources → Execution → Validation → Operational Handoff

Tickets are support requests. Missions are engineering work. A Help Desk ticket can reveal a larger problem and become a Mission Candidate, but the two records remain distinct.

Mission Proposal

Submit an engineered recommendation

Select an open mission. Your submission becomes a GitHub-backed proposal for Mission Command review. Do not include credentials, private student information, or sensitive production configuration.
