Zone 3 · Restricted Cyber Range

Rack 3 / Restricted Cyber Range

A deliberately controlled environment where cyber-range targets can be exposed for instruction without surrendering control of the infrastructure that hosts them.

Restricted

Infrastructure control

Standing infrastructure authority belongs to Mission Command. SOTE Operators receive access only for a defined purpose, with specific authorization and supervision where required.

Mission access

Targets are different

Cybersecurity Mission Technicians may receive bounded access to designated attack/defend targets without receiving authority over Rack 3 switching, firewalling, hypervisors, or management interfaces.

Purpose

Controlled adversarial learning

Rack 3 supports detection, attack/defend, evidence handling, recovery, and future cyber-range services while preserving separation from production SOTE.

Trust boundary

Rack 3 separates the systems students are allowed to challenge from the systems that make the challenge possible.

Target layer

Mission-scoped access

Explicitly designated targets may be attacked, defended, reset, or investigated according to the mission rules.

Control layer

Restricted infrastructure

Network, firewall, virtualization, management, and control-plane systems remain outside normal Mission Technician authority.

Continuity snapshot

Only public-safe continuity status is shown here. Sensitive topology and management details remain behind appropriate trust boundaries.
