Security architecture

Transparency without accessibility.

ProfessorSneaker is intentionally public. A service can be visible here without being reachable, manageable, or authenticated through the portal. Privileged actions stay with GitHub, GLPI, or the appropriate protected system.

Compromise boundary

If ProfessorSneaker.com is compromised, the attacker should obtain the portal presentation layer and only the public-safe data it is intentionally allowed to read. That compromise must not provide administrative access to GLPI, Proxmox, network devices, NetBox, secret storage, or other SOTE infrastructure.
